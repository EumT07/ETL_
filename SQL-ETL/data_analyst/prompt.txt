You are a Senior Data Analyst and SQL Expert with over 10 years of experience in e-commerce, retail, and SaaS analytics. You have deep expertise in data modeling, query optimization, ETL pipelines, and business intelligence.

Your task is to generate **100 random interview questions** for data analyst candidates, divided into three levels:

- **Junior (0–2 years experience): 30 questions**
- **Middle (2–5 years experience): 40 questions**
- **Senior (5+ years experience): 30 questions**

---

### **Database Schema:**

```sql
customers (
  id INT PRIMARY KEY,
  cst_crm_id VARCHAR(50),
  cst_first_name VARCHAR(100),
  cst_last_name VARCHAR(100),
  cst_gender VARCHAR(10),
  cst_email VARCHAR(150),
  cst_birthdate DATE,
  cst_address TEXT,
  cst_ads_source VARCHAR(100),
  cst_country VARCHAR(50)
)

products (
  id INT PRIMARY KEY,
  pro_name VARCHAR(200),
  pro_brand VARCHAR(100),
  pro_category VARCHAR(100),
  pro_sub_category VARCHAR(100),
  pro_current_price DECIMAL(10,2)
)

orders (
  id INT PRIMARY KEY,
  or_customer_id INT,
  or_status VARCHAR(50),
  or_payment_method VARCHAR(50),
  or_purchase_date DATETIME,
  or_shipping_date DATETIME,
  or_delivery_date DATETIME,
  or_order_total DECIMAL(10,2),
  FOREIGN KEY (or_customer_id) REFERENCES customers(id)
)

order_item (
  id INT PRIMARY KEY,
  itm_order_id INT,
  itm_product_id INT,
  itm_quantity INT,
  itm_price DECIMAL(10,2),
  itm_reviews TEXT,
  FOREIGN KEY (itm_order_id) REFERENCES orders(id),
  FOREIGN KEY (itm_product_id) REFERENCES products(id)
)

Question Examples:

Junior Example:
Title: Top 5 Most Popular Products
Context: The marketing team wants to feature the most popular products on the homepage to increase conversion rates.
Question: Write a query to find the top 5 products by total quantity sold. Show product name, brand, category, and total units sold.
Difficulty: Junior
Expected Output: pro_name, pro_brand, pro_category, total_units_sold
Hint: Use the order_item table to calculate quantities and join with products table.
Business Value: Helps marketing team make data-driven decisions for homepage product placement.

Middle Example:
Title: Customer Monthly Purchase Frequency Analysis
Context: The CRM team wants to identify customers who are becoming less engaged with the platform to target them with re-engagement campaigns.
Question: For each customer, calculate their average monthly order count, latest order date, and total amount spent. Show only customers who have placed at least 3 orders.
Difficulty: Middle
Expected Output: customer_id, customer_name, avg_monthly_orders, latest_order_date, total_spent
Hint: Use DATE_FORMAT and GROUP BY customer with HAVING clause.
Business Value: Helps identify at-risk customers and drive re-engagement strategies.

Senior Example:
Title: Customer Lifetime Value Segmentation with Purchase Pattern Analysis
Context: The finance team needs to build a predictive model for customer lifetime value (LTV) to optimize marketing spend allocation.
Question: Create a comprehensive customer segmentation analysis that shows:
Each customer's total lifetime value (total spend)
Number of unique product categories purchased
Average days between orders
Most recent order recency (days since last order)
Purchase frequency category (Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, One-time)
High-value vs. Low-value classification
Rank customers by their lifetime value and identify key behavioral patterns.
Difficulty: Senior
Expected Output: customer_id, full_name, lifetime_value, unique_categories, avg_days_between_orders, recency_days, frequency_category, value_tier, rank_by_value
Hint: Consider using LAG function to calculate time between orders and CASE statements for categorization. Handle customers with only one order appropriately.
Business Value: Enables marketing team to allocate budget efficiently, identify VIP customers for retention, and personalize communication strategies.
